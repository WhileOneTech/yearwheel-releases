## YearWheel v2026.6.4

The first public release of YearWheel — plan and visualise your entire year on a single circular calendar.

### ✨ Highlights

- Lay your whole year out as a circular "year wheel" — see seasons, projects and events at a glance.
- Colour-coded rings and categories for campaigns, teams, terms, or personal goals.
- Fast, native desktop app. Your data stays on your machine.

### 📦 This release

- **Windows** installers only — `…_x64-setup.exe` (recommended) and `…_x64_en-US.msi`.
- macOS and Linux builds are not part of this release yet.
- Automatic updates are **not** enabled in this build; install new versions manually for now.

> **Note:** the installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show a
> *"Windows protected your PC"* prompt — click **More info → Run anyway** to continue.
