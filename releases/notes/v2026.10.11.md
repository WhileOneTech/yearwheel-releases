## YearWheel v2026.10.11

The first macOS release — one download for both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs — with a rolling
view of the year, a redesigned Year Line, and the whole app now in Swedish and English.

### ✨ New

- **macOS build** for Apple Silicon and Intel in a single app.
- **Rolling time window:** centre the wheel on today (or any date) instead of January–December,
  showing 6, 12, 18 or 24 months. Plans that cross New Year read as one span, and New Year is
  clearly marked with the year named on each side. Step through time with a slider or the
  scroll wheel; **Today** jumps back.
- **Year Line redesigned** as the wheel laid flat: each ring is a thin lane running left to right,
  items keep their own colours, and today and New Year are drawn across every lane.
- **Remove duplicates:** when the same item appears more than once (for example holidays added
  twice), the item table offers to remove the extra copies. It asks first and can be undone.

### 🔧 Improvements

- The view switcher, time window and status filters stay at the top of the screen while you scroll.
- Every part of the interface is translated to Swedish and English, including the labels drawn
  on the wheel.
- Better contrast in dark mode, clearer keyboard focus and screen-reader labels.

### 🐞 Fixes

- Many smaller fixes to buttons and panels found in a review of the whole interface.
- The sample plan no longer shows behind dialogs on the Home screen.

### 📦 This release

- **macOS only.** Windows and Linux users: keep using
  [v2026.6.4](https://github.com/WhileOneTech/yearwheel-releases/releases/tag/v2026.6.4) for now.
- **Recommended:** `YearWheel-2026.10.11+1-macOS-universal.zip`. Unzip it and follow the
  installation guide inside (PDF, Swedish and English): in Terminal you run
  `install-yearwheel.command`, which copies YearWheel to Applications and opens it.
- The app isn't notarised by Apple yet, which is why the install script is needed. Opening the
  `.dmg` directly will make macOS warn that the app is from an unidentified developer.
- Automatic updates are **not** enabled; install new versions manually for now.
